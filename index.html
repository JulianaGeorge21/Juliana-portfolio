Project Scope & Objective

Designed to provide end-to-end analytical visibility into real estate sales operations, tracking customer installments, analyzing collected revenue, and identifying outstanding balances.

Detailed Contributions & Workflow

  • Data Modeling: Implemented a star-schema architecture linking a central fact table (Sales & Installments) with key dimensional tables (Properties, Clients, Dates) for optimized query speed and slice-and-dice analytics.

  • Executive Dashboard: Constructed an interactive layout featuring high-level KPI cards, temporal installment trends, and dynamic slicers for deep-dive exploratory analysis.
Technologies Applied: Power BI, Power Query, DAX, Dimensional Modeling (Star Schema), KPI Reporting.
